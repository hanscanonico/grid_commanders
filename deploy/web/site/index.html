A turn, in four orders

  1. Move

    Roads are cheap, woods are slow, and only foot soldiers climb mountains. Where you stop decides how much of the next shot you absorb.

  2. Fire

    Point at a target and the game shows the damage both ways before you commit. Then the exchange plays out up close.

  3. Capture

    Foot soldiers take cities, bases, airports and ports over a few turns. Every property you hold pays you each morning.

  4. Build

    Spend that income on new units at your bases, airports and ports. Then end your turn and see what the other side does about it.

A war of five flags

Five powers share one continent. A treaty has kept the peace for twenty years. The week it is due for renewal, the hall where it was signed burns down, and every army marches on the ruins.

The campaign runs for 39 missions across six acts. You command a different general most missions and fight under every flag, and what you do in one battle can change the next. A single run plays 36 of them.
