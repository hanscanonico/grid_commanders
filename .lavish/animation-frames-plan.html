Four clips exist and every one is exactly two frames. The grid is fixed: 18 unit columns × 6 faction rows of 64×96 cells; a new clip is always a new sheet pair, never new rows or columns (BattleView._last_seen_owner reads atlas y as the faction row).

The three defects in the current frames

  • Fighter and bomber are dead columns. Both build byte-identical voxel models for A/B and MOVE_A/MOVE_B; their whole animation is the composition-side 4 px bob (atlas.BOB_PX). Recorded in tests/measure_motion.py; the silhouette gate never catches it because translation moves every boundary texel.
  • The cast shadow reads as a detached black bar. A solid ellipse centred on column 34 (air 36) while the flip axis is 31.5. Infantry/mech body ends at y=84 at 4 px wide, y=85 is empty, then a 23 px near-black lozenge spans y=86–90. Tank's ellipse is 41 px at its widest under a hull whose last rows are far narrower.
  • KO'd cut-in figures read as blobs. There is no wreck art anywhere: a casualty is the idle figure under a flat WRECK_TINT (0.4) multiply + lift/spin/fade. The multiply collapses the interior toward the contour band that already covers 6–17% of every figure.

And one standing debt: the board legibility regression — the ruler reads 94.8% of clear cells failing at board resolution (16.0% on the pre-animation art), cause named in docs/sprite_legibility.md: the contour is not thick enough to survive nearest 4:1 sampling. The design record says answering it is the generator's. That is S8.

Locked — nothing below may move any of these

  • VOLLEY_POSE = 0.633, CUT_IN_POSE = 1.05, KO_POSE = 1.22, the aim-stretch ceiling (AIM_RATE_CEILING = 1.5, rate read once), the 2.90 s worst-exchange budget — all pinned by test_combat_beats.gd. New art changes which texture is drawn, never when.
  • anim.json is pinned, never read at runtime; test_anim_manifest.gd is its one consumer.
  • The cast shadow stays solid (measured; checkerboard retired) and the ambient pair stays off-centre — that asymmetry is what forbids a mirrored park and it is asserted from both sides (test_shadows.py, test_move_frames.gd). S3 reshapes the ellipse; it does not centre it.
  • The figure sheets only ever remove pixels from the board sheets (test_figure_sheet.gd). New poses are new clips beside the pair, not edits of it.
  • Effects stay procedural — "no explosion sheet" is fence D2 in cutscene_fx.gd. A unit pose (S4/S5) is unit art, not an effect sheet; the fence stands.
  • Instant is a still board and a skip lands on the final pose; the capture pin (BoardBeat.frozen) holds frame A. Every new animation obeys both.
  • Cadences may share no tick: any new period must not divide or multiply 500 / 160 / 900 at any tier.
  • Board-side dust under moving vehicles was measured and refused (generators/sprites/docs/move_dust.md) — do not re-propose it as sprite art.

1 · The slices

Ordered for value against effort. S1 and S7 are pure game code; S2–S5 and S8 are generator art; S6 is the one structural change. Each is independently shippable.

S1 — Wire the dead style scalars CODE S shipped

All eight data/battle_anim/*.tres already author dust (0.0–0.7) and arrive_scale (0.7–1.2), but CombatCutscene._style_pose hardcodes arrive_scale = 1.0; dust = 0.0 — a leftover from before the styles carried them. Delete the two literals and the cut-in gains, with zero new art:

  • Scuff puffs under arriving land units (_draw_scuff is fully built and currently dead).
  • Hulls roll in as one piece: _on_foot() keys off dust > 0.2, so tank/md_tank (0.5), artillery (0.7) and rocket carriers (0.4) stop trudging like infantry.
  • Per-weapon arrival depth: the bomber sweeps in from 1.2× out, small arms closes to 0.7×.

One wrinkle: recon (small_arms, dust 0.15) and anti_air (autocannon, dust 0.2) sit at or under the foot threshold, so both would still march. Same precedent as the artillery split (#622): give recon a pintle.tres split off small_arms with dust 0.3, and nudge autocannon to 0.25 — presentation keys only, no stat moves.

Fold in the four unfiled cut-in follow-ups while the file is open: (a) a kill's knock-back runs ~47 ms not 80 — CombatBeats._lost reports 0 for a dying side, needs a seam field; (b) muzzle_points() composes only _altitude, not roll/aim/lunge/brace — flash detaches up to 6 px; (c) anchor the flash on the drawn barrel mouth, not a fixed corner offset (reads oddest on the elevated howitzer); (d) the third casualty-cap step stays a playtest lever — hold.

Gates: test_combat_beats.gd untouched (timing does not move); test_battle_styles.gd unarmed mirror updated if autocannon/pintle change; cut-in smoke frames move — eyeball all 17.

S2 — Author fighter + bomber frame B ART M shipped

The two dead columns get real deltas in generators/sprites/spritegen/units/air.py (today neither has a beat(pose) branch at all):

  • fighter B: afterburner nozzle glow extends/brightens 2–3 texels, canopy glint slides one texel, elevons tick up. MOVE_B: burner plume lengthened a further texel over MOVE_A.
  • bomber B: nacelle exhaust flicker on the alternating engine pair, tail surfaces tick. MOVE_B: all nacelles lit + one texel of extra nose-down over MOVE_A's trim.

Author within the gates: ≥3 silhouette texels at rung 1 per livery, shimmer ≤5, mass drift ≤8%, shadow unmoved, and the "bob lifts the whole aircraft and nothing else" check must keep passing — the model delta has to be separable from the 4 px lift the way the copters' rotor tick is. Delete the stale note in tests/measure_motion.py and record the new numbers.

As built — two deviations from the art prose above, both forced by the legibility ratchet and measured rather than argued, and the check they replaced. (a) The canopy glint and the elevon/tail ticks are retones in place, never moved silhouette texels: both airframes run within a fraction of a ramp step of the bar on most grounds, and every spatial variant tried regressed 90–104 previously-passing baseline cells. As shipped the ratchet regresses 0. (b) The bomber's nacelle exhaust is gated on moving(pose) — MOVE_B only, all four lit — because any idle-beat placement cost the same two baseline cells (bomber meridian/gold, fog on plains). Its idle beat therefore carries the tail retones alone, sub-texel at board scale — and coincident in iron, whose ramp already holds that tone — so the board-scale idle read stays bob-only until S8 re-bakes the baseline. The burner spool is 0 / 2 / 1 / 2 courses of authored reach across rest / beat / move-held / move-beat, but the course at the nozzle mouths is occluded in every pose and livery, so only the outer one ever paints: the visible plume is 0 / 1 / 0 / 1 — one course on a beat, at rest or under way, and nothing between beats. (c) The old test_the_bob_lifts_the_whole_aircraft_and_nothing_else asserted the two models byte-identical — the dead-columns carve-out — so it could not survive this slice; test_the_bob_lifts_the_airframe_and_a_named_delta_besides replaces it, asking the models to differ and still share >90% of their voxels, the bob-subtracted cell to clear an IoU floor and to paint in at least one livery, and pose B to stay byte-identical to A from the ground line down. The move pair is held to the same bob-subtracted reading. The reasoning lives on units/air.py's beat(pose) branches and in tests/measure_motion.py; read one of them before touching either aircraft.

Gates: make sprites-test (AmbientFrames, MoveFrames), snapshot gate (make tiles in the same commit), test_ambient_frames.gd's every-column-differs pin now genuinely covers cols 9–10; legibility ratchet re-run. Both clips are held to the bob-subtracted airframe reading — the beat must paint in at least one livery and clear the IoU floor — so a column cannot go dead on the hop again.

S3 — Fit the cast shadow to the footprint ART M shipped

Reshape cell._shadow_ellipse so the shadow reads as contact, not a detached bar — keeping it solid and keeping the ambient pair's deliberate off-centre throw:

  • Close the vertical gap: for foot units the ellipse's top row must touch the body's contact row (today body ends y=84, shadow starts y=86).
  • Width from the footprint: derive the ellipse's width from the body's actual width over the ground rows per unit, instead of one formula that puts a 23 px lozenge under 4 px legs. Vehicles keep near-current widths; infantry/mech shrink hardest.
  • Air stays as is — the 12-row gap is the altitude cue.

Coupled constants: anim.json cell.ground_px is measured off the widest shadow row; if that row moves, UnitSprite.CELL_GROUND_PX (7) must move with it — test_anim_manifest.gd pins them together and the cut-ins centre their contact ellipse on it. Watch the rockets carrier's +0.37 px mirror margin against the 0.2 floor.

As built — the fit is a ceiling, not a free resize, and the depth stayed where it was. (a) Width is read from voxel.footprint_width, the model's lowest-z plane — what it plants on the ground — at a 0.41 coefficient, but never past the pre-S3 radius int(silhouette_w × 0.34): a footprint answer that widened a shadow the board had already been measured against turned a marginal reading over (apc pose B's mirror occlusion floor) rather than help. So all eight vehicles land byte-exactly on their old ellipse (rx unmoved, 14–21 px) and only the two foot units shrink: infantry 11 → 9 px, mech 11 → 8 px. (b) Only the WIDTH is the footprint's. Placement carries silhouette_w beside footprint_w because the ellipse's DEPTH still reads the whole crop — feeding the footprint into both axes took 27–38% of a vehicle's shadow AREA and regressed 429 previously-passing ratchet cells, measured and rejected. (c) The vertical gap closes on an ry floor of 3, so every foot ellipse now starts at y=85 rather than 86 — the row directly under the body's last, with no daylight left between them. The widest shadow row never moved, so the coupled constants are untouched: anim.json's ground_px and UnitSprite.CELL_GROUND_PX are both still 7, the rockets carrier keeps its +0.367 px mirror margin, and the two figures sheets are byte-identical — the subtraction erases every cast pixel whatever its shape. (d) The ratchet lands at 230 recovered / 1 regressed and the regression is accepted: board:idle_b:mech:iron:ready:port:fog falls 1.11 → 0.77 against the 1.0 fog bar, only the ry floor tips it, and every centre-shift alternative breaks the ground-row invariant or apc's occlusion test. The baseline is deliberately not re-baked — S8 owns it, and docs/sprite_legibility.md records the standing exception so a red ratchet is still readable. (e) No game code changed at all. tests/test_shadows.py::FootprintContact pins both halves — contact for every foot unit, and no land ellipse wider than the one it replaced — because the snapshot gate cannot catch either on its own, both sides of it coming from this same generator.

Gates: test_shadows.py (OneSun, CastShadow across rungs and parities, move lateral ≤0.5), test_figure_sheet.gd (tile shadow left behind in both frames, ground-line pin), snapshot gate, smoke frames.

S8 — Answer the legibility regression ART XL — its own programme shipped

Scheduled here (after S2/S3, before the new-pose slices) so the art re-baseline happens once. The board draws 64 px cells at 16 px with nearest filtering; the round-10 contour is mostly unsampled at that ratio, so 94.8% of clear board cells fail while the cut-in reads fine (21.6%). The fix is in the generator, and round 11 already candidate-measured the shape of it: a logical-pixel contour — 4 px lit side / 2 px ground side — thick enough that at 4:1 every sampling phase keeps at least one contour texel. Plus a value pass: 71% of failures are hue-carried (value-blind), so the interior ramp needs more value separation against the grounds, not new hues.

  • Work in spritegen/aa.py (contour pass) and palette.py outline modes; iterate against make legibility-check's frame axis and the worst-20 gallery.
  • Re-bake make legibility-baseline deliberately at the end; make legibility-ratchet is the reviewer of record, not Claude Design.
  • Interaction to hold: thickening contours worsens the WRECK/interior ratio the KO work (S4) cares about — land S8 first so S4 is authored on the final ramp.

As built — the work landed in spritegen/voxel.py, not in aa.py's contour pass or palette.py's modes, and the value half was a fallback rather than a re-ramp. (a) CONTOUR_DEPTH + _thicken_contour grow each already-classified 1px line into a band (4 lit, 2 ground-facing — round 10's own split) claimed entirely inward, so no alpha moves and every geometry reading (silhouette IoU, mass drift, the S3 shadow footprint) answers as it did. Gated to figures by is_unit (top_slot == S_RIM), which takes in the massif and leaves buildings, grounds and ramps byte-identical. (b) No new hues and no interior re-ramp: S8 measured that no row's ordinary sunward lift clears the board's ground band, so on a unit every grade falls to the ground-facing contour the way OUTLINE_HEAVY always did, and OUTLINE_RIM's climb is retired there (82-86% of aurora's and verdant's clear cells failed through the climb against 48-52% through the fallback). Off the board the grades are unchanged — applying the fallback to properties too cost 15 of 20 owner-pair readings. So OUTLINE_LIGHT, OUTLINE_HEAVY and OUTLINE_RIM now draw one and the same unit; all three are kept rather than renamed, and both generator docs say so. (c) One knock-on in aa.py: _safe refuses a corner write that would strand its only same-toned neighbour — a pre-existing bug the band exposed on rockets' rack — and settles to a fixed point rather than one pass (test_aa.NeverStrands). It moves no shipped pixel at MIN_RUN = 3. (d) The plan's 94.8% is the 2026-08-25 reading; S2/S3, the mountain peak and the wash had drifted the control to 86.1% before S8 touched anything, and that is what the results are stated against: clear 86.1% → 49.7%, fogged 80.6% → 33.8%, board over all four frames 88.2% → 52.5%, and the cut-in improved by an order of magnitude, 52.2% → 4.3% (the figure sheets are the board sheets minus the tile shadow, so the same band pays forward at 1:1). 17,672 cells FAIL → PASS against 18 the other way, all named in docs/sprite_legibility.md. (e) What resists is a GROUND value question this slice's scope excludes: dark liveries against mountain rock and property masonry (hq 74.3%, base 72.6%; missiles 86.5%, cruiser 79.4%) — round 10's own finding 5, recorded as the follow-up rather than tuned. (f) S3's accepted ratchet exception (board:idle_b:mech:iron:ready:port:fog) re-measures at 1.25 and is retired; the baseline was re-baked deliberately at the end (45,360 cells) and the ratchet is trivially clean against it. Test pins moved to the converged measurements and none was deleted — MAX_SUNWARD_DARK/MAX_CONTOUR, MAX_BLADE_DISSOLVE 0.55, and the bomber's ambient lit-floor waived for that one unit (its tail-tip retone is absorbed into the shared contour; the move clip still carries it).

Gates: legibility ratchet (the point of the slice), sprites-test full suite, snapshot gate, smoke sweep — expect nearly every frame to move; budget an eyeball pass.

S6 — N-frame plumbing, then a real gait and tread crawl CODE ART L

The only structural slice. The 2-frame assumption lives in one modulo (BoardBeat.frame_at's % 2) plus ternaries and literals downstream. Generalise first, then spend it:

  • Plumbing: frame_at(period_ms, elapsed_ms, frames := 2); UnitSprite's four sheet constants become per-clip arrays; generators side: Pose gains members, beat() becomes a frame index (restate the "frame-ticking branch reads the beat, not the pose" contract in index form), _tread_phase takes the index. anim.json needs no schema change — order derives from sheet count. Update the literal pins (test_anim_manifest both sides, LegibilityArt's frame axis).
  • Move clip 2→4 for foot units: contact-L / passing / contact-R / passing — a two-frame gait is a shuffle; four is a walk. 160 ms cadence unchanged (full cycle 640 ms).
  • Tread crawl: the 8-voxel track stripe steps quarter-phases across the four move frames instead of the current half-period flip.
  • Rotor: optionally a 4-tick disc on the same index (14° → 4 positions) — visible, cheap once the index exists.

Ambient stays two frames — at 500 ms the A/B breath reads fine and doubling every board sheet is cost without a complaint behind it.

Gates: every pinning suite named in §2 of the research grows; two new 1152×576 sheets; cadence-disjointness tests at every tier; smoke frames move.

S7 — The board moments that snap today CODE M, parallel-safe

Plus the two approved capture-backdrop notes if not folded into S1: dither the five horizon-glow band seams, and make tuft count scale with row area (clustered, seeded) instead of 13-per-row.

Gates: Instant = no-op everywhere; capture pin; smoke frames move on build/capture scenarios.
