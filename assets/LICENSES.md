## Typography — the two faces

The menu and commander-select screens are set in two OFL-licensed pixel faces,
vendored under `fonts/` and read through `scenes/common/ui_theme.gd` (`UiTheme`),
the menu's presentation authority (menu-revamp plan D2). Both were the "chosen
substitutes" the design-system handoff named because the repo shipped no UI font;
the substitution ends there — they are the game's faces now. The handoff's
Google-Fonts `@import` cannot ship in an engine build, so the TTFs are vendored
outright: it is the only form that works offline and in an export.

- **Pixelify Sans** — display and UI chrome. Vendored as the single variable TTF
  (`PixelifySans-Variable.ttf`); `UiTheme` asks it for its 700-weight instance for
  the wordmark and titles rather than carrying a second file.
  - **Copyright:** 2021 The Pixelify Sans Project Authors — <https://github.com/eifetx/Pixelify-Sans>
- **Silkscreen** — micro-labels, numerals, badges. The Regular and Bold cuts are
  vendored (`Silkscreen-Regular.ttf`, `Silkscreen-Bold.ttf`).
  - **Copyright:** 2001 The Silkscreen Project Authors — <https://github.com/googlefonts/silkscreen>

Both are licensed under the **SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1**; the full
licence text ships beside each family as `fonts/PixelifySans-OFL.txt` and
`fonts/Silkscreen-OFL.txt`. Antialiasing is switched off in `UiTheme` so the
glyphs rasterise on the same pixel grid the tile and unit art lives on.

## Air and naval sprites

The isometric aircraft and ship sprites (8 units × 5 team rows, 64×64 RGBA) are
original art created for this project, and the project owner holds the rights.
The neutral, red (Meridian), and blue (Aurora) rows — 24 PNGs — are hand-authored,
unlike the generated placeholder and portrait entries below. The 16 iron and
verdant rows are design-system faction tints (see below) derived from those red
rows, which keeps them project-original too. All 40 are vendored under
`sprites/units/` and replace the generated placeholders that previously occupied
those columns. Rebuild the atlas from them with `make tiles`.

## Missiles sprite

The Missiles launcher truck (1 unit × 5 team rows, 64×64 RGBA) is original
project art from the design-system handoff: a voxel-rendered cab + launcher body
+ twin raised tubes with a faction band, at the same 2:1 isometric projection,
ground line, and opaque `#53575e` ground shadow as the `apc`/`rockets` columns
beside it. It replaces the flat 16px placeholder silhouette that
`tools/generate_unit_placeholders.gd` drew into column 13 (that script now draws
nothing and only audits the atlas, cell by cell, for one no art reached). All 5
tints are vendored under `sprites/units/`.

## Airport and port building sprites

The airport and port buildings (2 buildings × 5 team rows, 64×64 RGBA, drawn to
match the PixVoxel style — the pack has no hangar and no quay) are the same
class of art under the same terms: neutral/red/blue hand-authored, iron/verdant
design-system faction tints derived from the red row. All 10 are vendored under
`sprites/iso_buildings/` and composited onto their generated grounds — an
asphalt apron and open water — by `tools/build_pixvoxel_atlases.sh`, replacing
the flat 16px generated art that previously drew those two tiles whole. The
city/base/hq iron and verdant buildings (6 PNGs, PixVoxel-derived and so CC0)
are vendored beside them and composited onto the paved lot the same way.

## Design-system faction tints

The iron (Iron Dominion) and verdant (Verdant League) rows of every unit and
property came from the Grid Commander design-system handoff (plan FI1's atlas
rows 3–4). Each is derived from the corresponding red-row sprite by remapping
only its team-coloured pixels onto a per-faction dark → base → light ramp (iron
`#090b0e → #2b3238 → #6e7b84`, verdant `#0e3d1a → #2c8636 → #8ede8c`); shared
detail pixels are byte-identical to the red row. They replace the first-cut
script tints (`tools/tint_iso_air_sea.sh`, since deleted) whose lighter iron
ramp read as neutral grey on the board. The license of each derived sprite
follows its red source: CC0 for the PixVoxel land units and city/base/hq
buildings, project-original for the hand-authored air/naval units and
airport/port. These are committed sources, not generated ones — there is no
script in the repo that reproduces them.

## Commander portraits — generated vector art

The portraits under `portraits/` are project-original and generated, not
vendored: `tools/commander_face_svg.gd` draws each general as an SVG — pose,
backdrop, prop, palette — and `tools/generate_portraits.gd` rasterises it. They
are the "Heroic Commander Portraits" design handoff, drawn to its spec rather
than exported from it, so no third-party pixels enter the tree. The only colours
that are not the handoff's own are the faction hues, read from
`CommanderVisuals`. Rebuild with `make portraits`.

## PixVoxel Revised Wargame Sprites

- **Author:** Tommy Ettinger
- **License:** CC0 1.0 — public domain dedication, no attribution required (we
  credit it anyway)
- **Source:** <https://opengameart.org/content/pixvoxel-revised-isometric-wargame-sprites>
- **Archive:** `Revised_PixVoxel_Wargame_1.7z` → `Revised_PixVoxel_Wargame/standing_frames/`

The pack is isometric-only, so its *ground* tiles (isometric elevation slabs)
are unusable on this square top-down grid and are not vendored. Only sprites
that sit **on** a tile — units and property buildings — are used, and they drop
into the existing grid unchanged.

Every sprite is the `Large`, `face0`, first standing frame, so the whole roster
shares one facing. Team rows come from the pack's palettes: **red = `color2`**,
**blue = `color6`**. Neutral is **`color1` desaturated to grey** — `color0`
(dark) is the pack's nominal neutral but its red trim reads as the red team.

### Vendored sources

The 36 source PNGs the atlases are built from — the 3 palettes above × the 9
units and 3 buildings mapped below, each `<palette>_<sprite>_Large_face0_0.png`
— are vendored under `sprites/pixvoxel_src/` (62 KB). CC0 permits this, and it
makes `make tiles` reproducible on a fresh clone with no download. The rest of
the pack is deliberately not vendored. A `.gdignore` there keeps Godot from
importing them as game assets; they are build inputs, not runtime resources.

Rebuild with `make tiles`, which uses those by default. To build from a full
extracted pack instead, override `PIXVOXEL`; see
`tools/build_pixvoxel_atlases.sh`.

### Unit mapping

The PixVoxel roster is smaller than ours and has no bazooka/scout/light-vs-heavy
tank/AA distinctions by name, so several entries are stand-ins chosen for a
unique silhouette. `_P`/`_S`/`_T` are the pack's weapon variants.

| Our unit | `atlas_col` | PixVoxel sprite | Match |
|---|---|---|---|
| infantry | 0 | `Infantry` | direct — rifleman |
| mech | 1 | `Infantry_T` | direct — the launcher-armed footsoldier |
| recon | 2 | `Supply_T` | **stand-in** — radar van; no scout/bike in the pack, picked for the mast silhouette |
| tank | 3 | `Tank` | direct — treads, single cannon |
| md_tank | 4 | `Tank_P` | **stand-in** — no light/heavy split; the bulkier twin-barrel variant |
| anti_air | 5 | `Artillery_S` | **stand-in** — no dedicated AA vehicle; twin tubes angled skyward |
| artillery | 6 | `Artillery` | direct — raised howitzer barrel |
| rockets | 7 | `Artillery_T` | direct — missile cluster |
| apc | 8 | `Supply` | direct — plain box van |

The pack stops there: it has no aircraft, no ships, no missiles truck, and no
iron or verdant palette, so every unit at `atlas_col` 9 or higher — and every
land unit's iron/verdant row — carries no pack-*rendered* pixels. Those cells
hold the vendored art above, re-pasted from `sprites/units/` by
`tools/paste_unit_sprites.gd`; the paste leaves the nine mapped columns'
pack-derived neutral/red/blue rows byte-identical.
