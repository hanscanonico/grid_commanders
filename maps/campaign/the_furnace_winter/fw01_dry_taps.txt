# Teaching board: the player owns only the HQ. Nothing is built until
# the crossroads base at the centre is taken — capture-before-production.

# The row-4 road is the race, and the starting cells are what make it one: the
# player's tank is 5 road tiles from the crossroads and Vale's is 9, so the
# player takes the ground and then holds it the two turns a capture costs.
# Do not put Vale's tank back on that road. From (10,4) it stands on the
# objective on day 1, and an infantry does 5 damage to a tank, so the only
# units that can capture have nowhere to walk and no way to shoot it off — the
# player was routed by day 3 in every seed measured, at every AI tier.
#
# The day-3 picket lands at (7,5) rather than on the road beside the base: by
# then the player's column is on row 4, and a spawn onto an occupied cell is
